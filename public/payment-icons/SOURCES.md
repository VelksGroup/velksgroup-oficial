# Payment brand assets

Official payment brand assets stored locally:

- `apple-pay.svg`: Apple Pay acceptance mark from https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/marketing/Apple-Pay-Mark.zip
- `link.svg`: official tintable Link lockup from https://github.com/stripe/stripe-ios/blob/master/StripePaymentSheet/StripePaymentSheet/Resources/StripePaymentSheet.xcassets/Link/link_logo_tintable.imageset/link_logo_tintable.svg, tinted with Link green `#00D66F`
- `klarna.svg`: https://osm.klarnaservices.com/images/badges/klarna_badge_rectangle.svg
- `amazon-pay.svg`: reversed acceptance mark for dark backgrounds from https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/amazonpayments/documentation/AmazonPay_BrandAssets/Logos/amazonpay-secondary-logo-rgb_rev.svg
- `stripe-wordmark-white.svg`: official white Stripe wordmark from https://stripe.com/newsroom/brand-assets

Preserve aspect ratios, colors and built-in clear space. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners.
